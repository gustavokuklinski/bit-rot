# Bit Rot
Developer: Gustavo Kuklinski

80% of human life had been wiped from a plage
Some archipealogo survived
Most of them were Brazilian vaccine and energy distribuitors, mostly petrol to the world
They were located in the middle of the pacific
The main company is Next Petrol, but some other companies were allowed to get resources and generate vaccines and provide energy.
But the vaccine gets a toll, your imune system is compromised by the rain. Your sickness will turn to infection.
There's a Military facility working on vaccine distribution and security of the whole
On a rescue mission they get an assynthomatic survivor and some people from the continent
They get infected
The island become a hell.

You have three missions: 
- 1. Die
- 2. Get the island safe
- 3. Contact the continent for a rescure

You you are at your own.

The player will have to survive the plague and rogues.
Theres the following companies working on the island: Gethulius, Exxoil, Rosskrild & Silva Family
They partned to create 'Next Petrol' on the following shares: 30% - Gethulius, 20% - Exxoil, 40% - Rosskrild & Silva Family and 10% public for who survived.
The player were a randomly third part employee doing a bit of everything directly contrated by Next Petrol. 
The player doesnt know they real employess to discover during the game.
The player will have the three missions using NPCs to create some quests
The quests will have some weight during the third mission
The gme world is randomly procedural, you will never player the same match twice
The player can create items and a base, choosing to just 'Get the island Safe' by surviving in the woods
The game will make the player get endless endings
